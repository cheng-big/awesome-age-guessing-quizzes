Awesome Age Guessing Quizzes (2026)

A curated list of age guessing quizzes, mental-age tests, and nostalgia games. The list includes daily games, short browser quizzes, and longer self-reflection tests.

Last checked: 2026-11-26 · Entertainment results are not age verification.

Daily age quizzes

  • Age of the Day — 10 daily questions · ~90 sec · free · no login · one shared puzzle for the world each day, with a streak, a result card, and friend challenges.
  • KEPIGAME — I Know Your Age — adaptive question count · free browser game · the game uses memories and cultural touchstones to produce an age guess.
  • Age Guesser on 100 Daily Games — variable question count · about 1–2 min · free · no account required · questions cover toys, games, gadgets, television, music, fads, and slang.

Nostalgia-based age tests

  • Freudly — Guess My Age Quiz — 70 questions · about 10 min · free · no sign-up needed to start · uses preferences, habits, media tastes, routines, and nostalgia.
  • ARealMe — Mental Age Test — 30 statements · about 3 min · free · no sign-up · returns a playful quiz age from 10 to 70 and shows the scoring rule.
  • BuzzFeed — 24-question Pop Culture Age Quiz — 24 yes-or-no questions · time varies · free to start · a pop-culture and nostalgia age guess.
  • BuzzFeed — 12-question Nostalgia Quiz — 12 questions · time varies · free to start · a nostalgia-based age guess.
  • ProProfs — How Old Am I? — 10 questions · time varies · free to start · a community quiz about mental age and everyday preferences.
  • ProProfs — What Is Your Mental Age Group? — 15 questions · time varies · free to start · a mental-age group quiz based on responsibilities, social life, and leisure.
  • BuzzQuiz — Mental Age Test — 12 questions · about 3 min · free · no sign-up · maps habits, playlists, and bedtime preferences to an age-vibe result.

Mental-age and lifestyle tests

  • Davemi — Mental Age Test — 20 questions · time varies · free · no sign-up · uses everyday situations about bedtime, technology, music, and money.
  • FastshotAI — How Old Do I Seem? — 6 questions · short format · free · no sign-up · estimates the age you seem from texting, sleep, music, slang, and snack habits.
  • MentalAgeCheck — Guess My Age Quiz — 10 questions · time varies · free to start · a text-based guess-your-age quiz based on habits, slang, and tastes.
  • Soulwisp — Guess My Age — 8–14 questions · time varies · a browser quiz that adapts follow-up questions around music, television, technology, and childhood memories.
