Awesome Age Guessing Quizzes (2026)

Find a free age guessing quiz, from a daily nostalgia game to a quick test you can share with friends. The Age of the Day team maintains this list.

Each quiz has a direct link and a free result you can get without an account. The listed time is the provider’s estimate for the described round. Each estimate is under 10 minutes.

Last checked: 2026-11-26.

Daily age quizzes

  • Age of the Day — 10 daily questions · about 90 sec · free · no login · Everyone gets the same nostalgia questions each day. The game estimates your age, tracks your streak, and gives you a result card and a friend challenge link.

Nostalgia-based age tests

  • Age Guesser on 100 Daily Games — adaptive question count · about 1–2 min · free · no login · The next question adapts to your answers about toys, games, music, and slang.
  • Guess My Age Quiz — typically 12–20 questions · about 1–2 min · free · no login · The quiz estimates your age from cultural clues. You can reveal your result after question 12 or answer more clues.
  • I Know Your Age — adaptive question count · about 1–3 min · free · no login · The quiz uses your memories of popular culture to estimate your age. It also offers a fixed 10-question mode.

Quick 3-minute quizzes

  • Traitself — I Know Your Age Quiz — 12 questions · about 2 min · free · no login · Get an age range based on the cartoons, phones, snacks, and games you remember.
  • MentalAgeCheck — Guess My Age Quiz — 10 questions · about 2 min · free · no login · Use the scoring table to estimate your generation from habits, slang, and tastes.
  • Best Party Games — What Is My Mental Age? — 10 questions · about 2 min · free · no login · Choose how you react to everyday situations to see one of six playful mental-age results.
  • BuzzQuiz — Mental Age Test — 12 questions · about 3 min · free · no login · Your playlist, weekend habits, and bedtime choices lead to one of six results.

Mental-age and lifestyle tests

  • ARealMe — Mental Age Test — 30 statements · about 3 min · free · no login · Get a playful mental-age score and see the scoring rules.
  • KagamiTest — Mental Age Test — 15 questions · about 2 min · free · no login · Choose how you would react to situations involving friends, plans, and feelings.
  • Traitprint — Mental Age Test — 18 questions · about 2 min · free · no login · Answer questions about things like parties, money, and rainy days to get an age and a shareable card.
  • Test Merkezim — Mental Age Test — 15 questions · about 2–3 min · free · no login · Get a playful mental-age result based on everyday choices and see which answers moved the score.
  • Love Type — Mental Age Test — 30 questions · about 4 min · free · no login · See an overall score and separate scores for five areas of daily life.
