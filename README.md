# Awesome Age Guessing Quizzes (2026)

A curated list of age guessing quizzes, mental-age tests, and nostalgia games. The list includes daily games, short browser quizzes, and longer self-reflection tests. The Age of the Day team maintains this list.

Inclusion rules are simple. The page must be publicly reachable, playable or readable without a paid subscription, and clear about what kind of age estimate it provides. A field says `varies` when the page does not publish one fixed value. Entertainment results are not age verification.

Last checked: 2026-11-26.

## Daily age quizzes

- **[Age of the Day](https://ageoftheday.com/)** — 10 daily questions · ~90 sec · free · no login · one shared puzzle for the world each day, with a streak, a result card, and friend challenges.

## Nostalgia-based age tests

- **[KEPIGAME — I Know Your Age](https://kepigame.com/games/nenrei.html?lang=en)** — adaptive question count · free browser game · the game uses memories and cultural touchstones to produce an age guess.
- **[Age Guesser on 100 Daily Games](https://100dailygames.com/games/age-guesser)** — variable question count · about 1–2 min · free · no account required · questions cover toys, games, gadgets, television, music, fads, and slang.
- **[BuzzFeed — 24-question Pop Culture Age Quiz](https://www.buzzfeed.com/morganmcfadden/yes-or-no-pop-culture-age-quiz)** — 24 yes-or-no questions · time varies · free to start · a pop-culture and nostalgia age guess.
- **[BuzzFeed — 12-question Nostalgia Quiz](https://www.buzzfeed.com/audreyworboys/this-nostalgia-quiz-will-accurately-guess-your-age-with)** — 12 questions · time varies · free to start · a nostalgia-based age guess.

## Mental-age and lifestyle tests

- **[Freudly — Guess My Age Quiz](https://freudly.ai/tests/guess-my-age-quiz/)** — 70 questions · about 10 min · free · no sign-up needed to start · uses preferences, habits, media tastes, routines, and nostalgia.
- **[ARealMe — Mental Age Test](https://www.arealme.com/mental/en/)** — 30 statements · about 3 min · free · no sign-up · returns a playful quiz age from 10 to 70 and shows the scoring rule.
- **[ProProfs — How Old Am I?](https://www.proprofs.com/quiz-school/story.php?title=pq-how-old-am-i-mentally)** — 10 questions · time varies · free to start · a community quiz about mental age and everyday preferences.
- **[ProProfs — What Is Your Mental Age Group?](https://www.proprofs.com/quiz-school/story.php?title=what-is-your-mental-age-group)** — 15 questions · time varies · free to start · a mental-age group quiz based on responsibilities, social life, and leisure.
- **[BuzzQuiz — Mental Age Test](https://www.buzzquiz.io/mental-age-test)** — 12 questions · about 3 min · free · no sign-up · maps habits, playlists, and bedtime preferences to an age-vibe result.
- **[Davemi — Mental Age Test](https://www.davemi.com/tests/mental-age-test)** — 20 questions · time varies · free · no sign-up · uses everyday situations about bedtime, technology, music, and money.
- **[FastshotAI — How Old Do I Seem?](https://fastshotai.com/c/guess-my-age-quiz)** — 6 questions · short format · free · no sign-up · estimates the age you seem from texting, sleep, music, slang, and snack habits.
- **[MentalAgeCheck — Guess My Age Quiz](https://mentalagecheck.com/guess-my-age-quiz/)** — 10 questions · about 2 min · free · no sign-up · a self-scored quiz about habits, slang, and tastes.

## Contributing

Add one entry through a pull request. Use this format and keep the fields factual.

```text
- **[Name](https://example.com/quiz)** — question count · time · access · one sentence describing the quiz.
```

Please include the exact quiz URL, check the page before opening the pull request, and state when a field varies or is not published.
